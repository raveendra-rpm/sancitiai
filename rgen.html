When the Tune No Longer
Matches the System.

Systems evolve through years of patches, releases, and quick fixes until what is documented no longer reflects what is running. The knowledge needed to close that gap is often scattered across codebases, documents, meeting minutes, and the people who remember why a decision was made.  Without a current source of truth, every downstream decision: modernization, code generation, testing, compliance, and release, starts with avoidable guesswork.

  • Requirements are fragmented across code, documents, meetings, and ticketing systems
  • Critical business logic is implicit, undocumented, or retained only as tribal knowledge
  • Manual requirements capture slows delivery and creates inconsistent interpretation
  • Disconnected requirements break traceability between business intent, code, testing, and release

RGEN reads these sources together, turning silent logic and scattered inputs into a current score the whole delivery system can follow.
