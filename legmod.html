When Legacy Complexity
Falls Out of Sync.

Modernization is rarely a code problem alone. The real challenge is knowing what to preserve, what to change, what depends on what, and how to prove the modernized system still performs as intended.

  • Business logic is buried in aging code, stored procedures, documents, and tribal knowledge
  • Technical debt consumes capacity long before teams can fund new value
  • Application dependencies make isolated, file-by-file upgrades difficult to govern
  • Big-bang rewrites force all-or-nothing decisions around systems the business cannot afford to disrupt

LegMod turns that uncertainty into a modernization plan built around managed waves, not one high-risk leap.
